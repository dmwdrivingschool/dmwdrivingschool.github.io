📱 The Drive My Way app

Watch your instructor arrive, see your lessons and credit, message your instructor, follow your progress and practise for your theory test, all on your phone.

Free for every DMW Driving School pupil

The Android app is going live on Google Play very soon.

Every image below is a genuine screenshot of the pupil app. The names, lessons, messages and figures are sample data on a demo account. No real pupil's information appears on this page.

Live tracking

🗺️ Watch your instructor arrive

When your instructor sets off, your app switches to a live map showing where they are and when they'll get to you. No more waiting at the window wondering if they're nearly there.

  • Your instructor's position on a map, updating as they drive
  • An arrival time the moment they set off
  • A message the moment they arrive
  • The map closes by itself when your lesson starts

Real screenshot of the pupil app while the instructor is on the way.

Your lessons

📅 Your lessons, offers and credit in one place

Your home screen shows your upcoming lessons and how many hours of credit you have. If a lesson slot comes up, your instructor can offer it to you, and you accept or decline with one tap.

  • Upcoming lessons at a glance
  • Accept or decline lesson offers straight away
  • Your hours of credit on your home screen

Real screenshot of the pupil home screen with a lesson offer.

Holidays & availability

🗓️ Tell us when you're away, see when your instructor is

Add your holiday in the app and it shows in your instructor's diary straight away, so nothing gets booked while you're away. When your instructor is away, you see it on your home screen too.

  • Add or change your own holidays
  • Your instructor's away dates shown clearly
  • Cancel a lesson with one tap, and it's confirmed straight away in your app
  • Let your instructor know which times suit you

Real screenshot of the pupil home screen with a holiday booked.

Messages

💬 Messages that don't get lost

Message your instructor in the app, send photos, and get a notification when there's a reply. Anything important pops up when you open the app, so you never miss it.

  • Chat with your instructor, with photos
  • Notifications for new messages and lesson changes
  • Important messages you can't scroll past

Real screenshot of messages in the pupil app.

Theory test

🎓 Theory practice and mock tests

Practice questions by topic, mock tests marked like the real thing, and road signs to learn, with your progress saved as you go so you can see what still needs work.

  • 50-question mock tests with the real pass mark of 43
  • Practise by topic and see what you got right and wrong
  • Road signs and what they mean

Real screenshots of theory practice and road signs.

Learning videos

🎬 Videos to watch between lessons

Short videos on moving off, junctions, roundabouts, manoeuvres and more, so you can go over what you've learnt before your next lesson.

  • Grouped by topic, in the order you'll learn them
  • Watch any time, as often as you like

Real screenshot of the learning videos.
