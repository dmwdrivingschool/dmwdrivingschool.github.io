🚗 Welcome to DMW Driving School

Grade A Manual Driving Tuition in Gloucester & Surrounding Areas

At DMW Driving School, we don’t just teach you to pass your practical test—we give you the lifelong confidence and skills required to stay safe on the road for life.

As a Grade A Approved Driving Instructor, I pride myself on delivering a calm, patient, and highly enjoyable learning environment. Whether you are sitting behind the steering wheel for the very first time or looking for a structured path to test day, my relaxed approach is designed to make learning to drive fun.

🎬 Access To Our Interactive Video Lesson Portal

Get exclusive, free access to our private training resource hub between our sessions. This comprehensive suite of 3D instructional videos covers all key aspects of the DVSA syllabus, serving as the perfect visual tool to solidify exactly what you have learned behind the wheel:

  • 🛣️ Core Driving Essentials – Master the early foundational routines, clutch control mechanics, and standard road positioning principles.
  • 🔄 Roundabouts & Junctions – Study exact lane disciplines, T-junction approach lines, and complex spiral roundabout exits.
  • ┼ Crossroads & Lights – Clear structural blueprints for navigating tricky offside/nearside turns and yellow box junctions safely.
  • 🅿️ Practical Test Manoeuvres – Step-by-step 3D walk-throughs for parallel parking, forward/reverse bay parking, and steep hill starts.
