Interactive AI Driving Guides & Core Lessons

1. Junctions & Emerging

Key Points: Apply the M-S-M routine early. Assess zoning speed. Look right, left, and right again. Creep out with clutch control if lines of sight are limited.

2. Roundabouts & Lane Discipline

Key Points: Check mirrors and signal early. Give way to traffic already approaching from the right. Maintain solid positioning inside your lane markings.

3. Dual Carriageways & Slip Roads

Key Points: Build up speed on the slip road to match main traffic flows. Check your right blind spot quickly. Merge smoothly without disrupting passing vehicles.

4. Overtaking & Lane Changing

Key Points: Confirm a safe following distance ahead. Perform mirror checks to verify rear clearance. Signal clearly, change lanes smoothly, and cancel the indicator prompt promptly.

5. Pedestrian Crossings & Hazards

Key Points: Scan walking paths and pavements early. Look for pedestrians approaching boundaries. Check your interior mirror before braking gently.

6. Driving in Adverse Weather

Key Points: Double your total stopping gap on wet roads. Use dipped headlights in low visibility. Ease off the gas pedal smoothly if tires begin to slide or aquaplane.

7. Independent Driving & SatNav

Key Points: Focus heavily on road signage rather than guessing directions. Listen to GPS prompts well in advance. Remain calm if you misinterpret a turn.

8. Emergency Stop Procedure

Key Points: Secure a solid grip on the steering wheel. Apply immediate and heavy brake force. Push the clutch all the way down just before coming to a full stop. Securing parking brake immediately.
